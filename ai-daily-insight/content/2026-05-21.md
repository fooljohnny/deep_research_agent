🔴 重大突破

例如，[POLAR-Bench: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Privacy-Utility Trade-offs in LLM Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19127) 和 [GraphDiffMed: Knowledge-Constrained Differential Attention with Pharmacological Graph Priors for Medication Recommendation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20188)。
